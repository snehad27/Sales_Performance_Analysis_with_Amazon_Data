# Sales_Performance_Analysis_with_Amazon_Data
The analysis utilizes a comprehensive dataset of Amazon sales transactions spanning a specific period (January 2020 to December 2024) across various regions and product categories. Key data attributes include Order ID, Order Date, Product Name, Category, Quantity Sold, Unit Price, Total Sales Amount, Payment Method, and Order Status.

## Tools used
* Excel for data cleaning
* Tableau for data Visualization.
## Key Insights and Recommendations

The analysis utilizes a comprehensive dataset of Amazon sales transactions spanning a specific period (January 2020 to December 2024) across various regions and product categories. Key data attributes include Order ID, Order Date, Product Name, Category, Quantity Sold, Unit Price, Total Sales Amount, Payment Method, and Order Status.

After cleaning the data, the cleaned data source is loaded to the tableau for visualization. Visualizing the data based on different KPI for the sales in the US conveying the following insights.

* Total sales aggregated to ~$91.83M from January 2020 to December 2024.Whereas total number of units sold in the same duration is 0.30M with total 1 lakh number of orders.
* Month wise sales trend is showing volatility across the months with steady dip in February and rise in March. The highest rise in sales in March 2020 that is 18.50% but also the highest dip in February 2020 that is 18.18%. The rise and dip is consistent over the year for the same month which is indicating the seasonal rise and drop. Despite the short-term volatility the long-term sales are rising over the period.
* Average order value for each category is greater than Median order value, which is representing that some customers are buying the high value items (expensive items) making the overall data positively skewed.
* Nearly half of our total sales (44.74%) are concentrated within Texas and California, underscoring our dominant presence in these two key regions.
* Category wise electronics items have the largest sales of ~$49.70M with highest number of units sold (i.e. ~1.62 lakh) while the books have the lowest sales of ~$3.54M with lowest number of units sold(i.e. ~0.11 lakh).
* Payment wise approximately 35.04% of people are preferring the credit card, whereas the number of people preferring COD remains low to 4.93%.
The clothing and toys category showing more AOV and MOV than Electronics means customers are buying more expensive items in these two categories and buying more but low cost items from the electronics category.
